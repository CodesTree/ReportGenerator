# Research Pipeline

Requirement knowledge only; no pipeline design or implementation is approved.
Collect papers, reputable news, official announcements, technical community
discussions, and accessible newsletters daily; synthesize weekly, analyse trends
monthly, and publish quarterly subject to human approval. Deduplicate related
reporting while retaining historical research and source provenance.

Dependencies: [[memory/concepts/claim-verification]] checks evidence;
[[memory/concepts/editorial-engine]] selects and develops stories. Source access,
cadence boundaries, and retention remain open. External research is untrusted.
