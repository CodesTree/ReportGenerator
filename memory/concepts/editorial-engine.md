# Editorial Engine

All five categories are permanent. Measure balance by substantive editorial word
count: 15% minimum target and normal 25% maximum per category; aim for three
meaningful subtopics per category per quarter. Apply a configurable 20% threshold
for a single organization. Document exceptions and obtain human approval.

Rank stories by technical significance 30%, industry/societal impact 25%, evidence
credibility 20%, novelty 15%, and quarterly relevance 10%. Scales and attribution
rules remain unresolved.

Every significant article explains what happened, why it matters, how it works,
limitations, and what comes next. Start accessibly, then add technical depth;
avoid jargon-heavy, sensational, or promotional prose. Articles must be original
and technically accurate.

Relationships: [[memory/concepts/research-pipeline]] supplies research;
[[memory/concepts/claim-verification]] constrains factual claims;
[[memory/concepts/word-generation]] presents the approved editorial content.
